Flight Booking Policy

Refund Policy

All flights are refundable according to the following conditions:

  • Cancellations made more than 24 hours before departure: Full refund
  • Cancellations made within 24 hours of departure: 50% refund
  • No-shows: No refund

Baggage Policy

Standard baggage allowance includes:

  • Carry-on: 7kg
  • Checked baggage: 30kg
  • Additional baggage: BDT 500 per kg

Check-in Policy

Passengers must check in:

  • Online: 24 hours to 1 hour before departure
  • At the airport: At least 2 hours before domestic flights, 3 hours before international flights

Changes and Modifications

Flight changes are subject to availability and may incur additional fees:

  • Date changes: BDT 1,500 per person
  • Route changes: BDT 2,500 per person
  • Name changes: Not permitted

Special Assistance

Passengers requiring special assistance should notify us at least 48 hours before departure. This includes:

  • Wheelchair assistance
  • Special meals
  • Medical equipment

Travel Insurance

We recommend purchasing travel insurance to cover unexpected events such as:

  • Trip cancellation or interruption
  • Medical emergencies
  • Lost or delayed baggage
  • Flight delays or cancellations

Terms and Conditions

By booking with us, you agree to the following terms:

  • All passengers must carry valid travel documents
  • It is the passenger's responsibility to ensure they meet visa requirements
  • The airline reserves the right to change schedules without prior notice
  • Passengers should arrive at the airport at least 2 hours before domestic flights and 3 hours before international flights
